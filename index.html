Broadcasting with mobile agents

Grid Broadcast

Agents live on the vertices of a graph: a grid Pm × Pn, a graph you draw on up to seven vertices arranged in a circle, or a bipartite graph inside Km,n. Each round the tree player chooses a spanning tree, then the agent player moves agents along its edges. Whenever agents share a vertex and one of them is knowledgeable, they all become knowledgeable.
